Research

Quantum Error Correction and Quantum Simulation

Analysis of quantum error correcting codes and error suppression protocols in quantum systems, including computers and sensors. Study of molecules and material chemical and physical properties with quantum computers.

QEC Fault-Tolerance Quantum Metrology Quantum Simmulation

Condensed Matter Physics & Materials Science

Magnetism in 2D materials usually vanishes well below room temperature. We look for the exceptions: we search their compositional space with first-principles simulations, evaluating magnetic exchange, magnetocrystalline anisotropy and Dzyaloshinskii–Moriya interaction to find candidates that sustain magnetic order in devices a few atoms thick.

Research Team

We are a theoretic-computational group at University of Costa Rica working on quantum computing, mainly: quantum error correction, simulation of chemical systems, as well as the study of magnetic systems through first principle simulations, with the goal of developing more robust and efficient quantum technologies.
